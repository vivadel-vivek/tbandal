# Readers

You don't have an account. You probably won't make one. That's fine —
the editorial site works completely without sign-in.

## What you can do without an account

- Read every published review.
- Read every published journal post.
- Browse the full tea catalog with all filters (type, vendor, region, beginner-mode buckets).
- Read the glossary, methodology page, and brewing primers.
- Walk through `/start` to get a brewing recipe tailored to a tea you already own.
- Click "Visit shop" on any vendor — outbound redirects through `/go/[vendor]` so the vendor knows you came from us.

## Pages worth knowing

### Home (`/`)
The masthead. Recently brewed teas, featured journal posts, and
the methodology pitch. Also the only page where you'll see the
hero treatment.

### Journal (`/journal`)
Long reads, brewing notes, and vendor spotlights. Each post is
written by Vivek or James with a single, identifiable voice — no
guest contributors, no SEO listicles. If a post mentions a tea
you've not heard of, the catalog is one click away.

### Tea catalog (`/discover/teas`)
The core of the site. Two view modes:

- **Standard** — type filters by name (Green, White, Sheng Pu'er,
  Shou Pu'er, Dark, etc). Hover any chip to see a one-line
  definition (helpful if you don't know what "Sheng Pu'er" means
  yet).
- **New to tea** (toggle in the top-right) — friendlier buckets:
  Light & floral, Layered & complex, Bold & roasted, Earthy & aged.
  Same teas, less jargon.

### Tea detail (`/tea/[vendor]/[slug]`)
Each tea page has:

- A hero block with the tea's photo, name, vendor, region, and our
  rating.
- A 6-axis (Basic) or 12-axis (Advanced) flavor radar, toggleable.
- A mouthfeel grid showing astringency and body.
- Reviews from Vivek and James, each on their own tab, with a
  member-aggregate tab if any members have logged a session.
- Recommended brewing parameters — leaf weight, water temperature,
  first steep time, ramp guidance, and (where it matters) rinse
  count.
- Related teas if you want to keep going.

### Glossary (`/discover/glossary`)
Every tea word a newcomer might trip on. Includes cross-references
between East/West naming pairs:

- Black tea ↔ Hong cha (红茶) ↔ Red tea
- Hei cha (黑茶) ↔ Dark tea
- Longjing ↔ Dragonwell
- Tieguanyin ↔ Iron Goddess

If you see one of these terms in a review, hovering it reveals an
inline popover with the definition.

### Guided brewing (`/start`)
A four-question flow: what category of tea do you have, what gear
do you own, what shape of cup are you going for, and what does
"strong" mean to you? You get a recipe — leaf weight, water temp,
first steep, total steeps, ramp guidance — plus an in-page timer
that walks you through the session.

### How we rate (`/how-we-rate`)
The methodology page. Explains the flavor wheel, the mouthfeel
grid, the 0-10 / 0-5 scales, the "Basic" / "Advanced" view toggle,
and the recommendation engine boundaries (no sheng → shou
crossover unless you've rated both, soft aged-status nudges).

### About (`/about`)
Two-person team page. Vivek's palate signature (subtle, malty,
nutty register; aligned with aged whites and Yunnan blacks).
James's palate signature (the tea lead — sheng pu'er and the aged
corner of the cabinet).

## Privacy

When you arrive for the first time you'll see a small notice at
the bottom-right of the page explaining that we use first-party
storage to remember your preferences. No cookies for tracking, no
third-party analytics, no advertising. Click "Got it" to dismiss
and it won't return on this device.

You can read the full policy at `/privacy` — it's written in plain
English and takes about two minutes.

## When you're ready for an account

Members get a personal library, a session log that ties back into
the recommendation engine, the ability to share specific brewing
sessions, and palate alignment. None of those features change the
public site you've been reading. Sign-up is at `/signup`.
